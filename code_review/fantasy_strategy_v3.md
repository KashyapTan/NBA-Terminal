# V3 engine review and model contract

Scope: pure optimizer, snapshot adapter and focused synthetic tests. CODE_REVIEW_GUIDE.md is absent at accepted base 784a7e4; AGENTS.md review criteria applied directly.

## Behavior and findings resolved

- Recommendations preserve every improving explored add/drop pair (best transaction date per pair). No unique-add restriction, seven-result cap, forced diversity or padding. Groups expose ranks by drop ID. Repeated drops represent alternatives; only `plan` is a sequential ledger.
- All legal normalized roster players are considered, including first-round/core players. Only explicit Protect, known legal undroppable and known locked remain hard constraints. Sustained per-game replacement cost remains a visible heuristic deduction; draft metadata is shown as a tradeoff. Tiny gains that do not pay that cost produce zero suggestions.
- The selected-five objective is the mean uncalibrated category win estimates for a fixed selected five, minus sustained-value cost. It is not the nine-category probability of five wins. `probability_gain` separately retains the latter diagnostic. Current actuals, opponent remaining playable schedule/rates and historical margin variation determine category estimates. Default selection starts with projected category strength and includes observed candidate-rate upside as reachability guidance. Explicit five-key preferences override it. Candidate upside is a selection heuristic; each recommendation is then checked against a full lineup forecast with lost starts and opportunity costs.
- Favored categories count toward five and expose `safe_win`, while only selected non-safe categories are improvement targets. Saturated safe wins supply negligible marginal utility; close margins remain sensitive. Normal approximations are uncalibrated and should not be presented as precise odds. Opponent baseline lineup is frozen during pair comparisons, preventing apparent gains from changing hypothetical opponent choices.
- `calculation` supplies rates, eligible scheduled games, projected starts, added/lost totals and other-lineup effects. Count `net_total = add_rate*add_starts - drop_rate*drop_starts + lineup_other_net`. `gains` is the actual full-team delta. Percentages expose weighted team makes/attempts before and after; percentage averages are never subtracted as counting totals. TO lower wins.
- Forecast starts from immutable recorded actuals. Only remaining unstarted games contribute; adapter reserves started active slots. Horizon supplies as-of timestamp, final matchup date and remaining dates. Daily acquisition ledgers, same-day transaction ordering and waiver clearance remain unsupported and explicitly conditional.
- Optional `matchup_add_limit_fallback` is accepted only with validated league/team/matchup context, absent ESPN cap, explicit complete matchup usage and supported matchup rules. No default seven exists in engine. UI persists user-authorized cap by league/season and sends it in current context. Six complete matchup acquisitions plus scoped cap seven yields one available; season transaction totals never substitute for weekly usage.

## Bounds and remaining risks

Daily lineup matching is greedy by marginal selected-five utility, then position-compatible augmentation; it does not exhaustively optimize every lineup. Multi-move plans use bounded beam search, maximum four stream roots, at most fourteen dates and 20,000 pure-engine evaluations (adapter 5,000). Up to 1,000 normalized players/candidates are accepted. Within each date, candidate iteration rotates through all legal drops before the next candidate, avoiding spending the entire budget on one drop. `search` discloses pool, evaluated/legal drop IDs, candidate/evaluation bounds, incomplete search and returned alternatives. `alternatives_complete` concerns explored one-move timing/pairs, not globally optimal lineups or sequential plans. A bound may omit beneficial options; no fixed output cap disguises it.

Missing candidate schedules/rates exclude only that candidate and retain targeted provenance. Missing roster/opponent contributions still prevent a confident full-team plan. IR activation is not modeled. Source rates/schedules/locks are adapter inputs and may be stale. No network, authentication, roster writes or frontend code was changed. Real ESPN validation remains the integrator's separate consenting test boundary.
